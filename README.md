# Milad Morad

I am an AI researcher at Fraunhofer FIT and a doctoral candidate at RWTH Aachen University. I study how human expertise, feedback and work practices can inform the evaluation and adaptation of AI systems.

My research connects human–AI interaction, measurement development and professional requirements for agentic systems.

## Research software

**[Grounded Reflection](https://github.com/miladmo/grounded-reflection)** is an early research prototype for evidence-grounded reflection and scoped agent adaptation. I developed its research concept and evaluation design. The repository includes evidence models, executable checks, tests and a documented model pilot.

**[HumanTrust](https://github.com/miladmo/humanmark-builder)** is a browser interface prototype exploring pledges and badges for human-centered content creation.
